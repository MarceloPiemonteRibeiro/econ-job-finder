# Spontaneous-application target list — Marcelo (JM 2026-27)

**What this is.** Where to send *proactive / cold* applications and expressions of
interest — the places where a well-aimed email + CV lands, not just formal vacancies.
Formal openings at these same institutions are already caught by `watcher.py`; this
list is for the roles that are filled through networks and unsolicited approaches.

**How to use.**
- Work top-down by tier (your location preference: CH > W-Europe > rest of Europe > Asia/US > LatAm > Africa).
- Fill the **Contact** column yourself from your advisor's network / seminar contacts — a warm intro beats a blind email. Don't cold-email a generic address if you can help it.
- **Fit angle** = the hook to lead your email with, grounded in your work (JMP on political lending / sovereign credit; firm-level energy; political economy of reform). Frame it as *their* agenda meeting your evidence, not a pitch of invented projects.
- Review quarterly; when a target opens a formal role, it'll come via the daily digest.

**Legend.** `[★]` = you flagged as interesting in the career-center sheet. Approach: **email** = unsolicited CV/cover works · **PI** = email a specific chair/researcher · **portal+network** = apply formally *and* network in.
